# *Typography*: one sans, one serif accent

The interface speaks in **Inter Tight**. Warmth comes from a single italic serif phrase, a marker swipe or a handwritten word, never from a second sans. Numbers that matter are set in the serif.

## The families

<FontCards />

All faces are free to use. Inter Tight and Instrument Serif load from Google Fonts in the documentation; the apps bundle them so they work offline.

:::note A decision recorded
The Platform prototype was drawn in **Geist**. The brand system and the Manager and Mobile prototypes use **Inter Tight**, so Inter Tight is the product face and Geist is a prototype artifact. The two have close metrics; no screen needs redrawing.
:::

## The headline recipe

A headline is a sans sentence with **one** emphasized phrase in the serif italic. Add the marker swipe or the script only when the line has room for a third voice.

| Device | Face | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emphasis phrase | Instrument Serif, italic | One per heading, 1 to 4 words |
| Marker swipe | Amber band behind the text | One per view; teal or mint on request |
| Script | Nothing You Could Do, underlined | One word per screen, display sizes only |
| Eyebrow | Inter Tight 600, caps, 0.18em | Above a heading, 12 to 13 px |
| Big figure | Instrument Serif, upright | The one number a card is about |

## The ramp

The brand ramp runs from 12 to 72 px. Product screens live between 12 and 32: tables and dense UI at **14 px**, body at 16.

<TypeRamp />

Display sizes are fluid for marketing and the Hub: `--text-display-xl` (up to 120 px), `-lg` (80) and `-md` (56), each a `clamp()`.

### Weights and leading

| Weight | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 400 | Body, long text |
| 500 | Labels, chips, table cells that need weight |
| 600 | Buttons, field labels, headings in product |
| 700 | Rare: marketing statements only |

Leading is `0.95` for stacked display lines, `1.1` to `1.15` for headings, `1.6` for body. Keep paragraphs to **60 to 75 characters** per line.

## Numbers

Tables and stats use **tabular numerals** (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so columns align. Align numbers right. A stat's figure is the serif; its unit and label stay sans.

## Do and don't

<DoDontRow>
  <DoDont kind="do">Use the serif for one phrase in a heading, or for one figure in a card.</DoDont>
  <DoDont kind="dont">Set body copy, labels, buttons or navigation in the serif.</DoDont>
  <DoDont kind="do">Write interface text in sentence case.</DoDont>
  <DoDont kind="dont">Use Title Case, ALL CAPS beyond eyebrows, or fake bold.</DoDont>
</DoDontRow>
