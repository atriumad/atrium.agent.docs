# *Foundations*: the shapes behind every screen

## Spacing

An **8 px grid** with a 4 px half step. Space between related things is small, space between groups is large. The brand breathes: when in doubt, add space.

<SpaceScale />

| Where | Gap |
| --- | --- |
| Icon to label | 4 to 8 px |
| Label to field | 6 to 8 px |
| Between fields | 16 px |
| Card padding | 20 px in product, 28 px in marketing |
| Between cards | 16 px |
| Between page sections | 48 to 64 px |

## Layout

Content stops at **1200 px** (1360 for wide dashboards). The side gutter is `clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 4rem)`. The Manager and the Platform show a side navigation from `lg` upward; below it the navigation moves into a menu.

<Breakpoints />

Design mobile first. A table that cannot fit becomes a list of cards, never a horizontal scroll of the page.

## Radius

Pills for what you press, soft cards for what you read, 4 to 8 px for fields.

<RadiusScale />

## Borders

Pills and outlines are drawn with a **1.5 px** stroke, never a heavier one. Hairlines on cards and tables are 1 px in `border-light`. On dark, hairlines are `border-on-dark`.

## Elevation

The identity is flat. Elevation is **color**: a mint or amber field, a white card on a cloud page. Shadows exist only for things that float.

<ShadowScale />

| Use a shadow for | Do not use one for |
| --- | --- |
| Menus and popovers (`shadow-pop`) | Cards in a grid |
| Dialogs, hover lift (`shadow-float`) | Buttons |
| Bento tiles on an aurora field (`shadow-soft`) | Table rows, inputs |

### Aurora and grain

Marketing and the Hub may use the **aurora**: a pale mint and amber wash from the corners, with dark type on top. It is secondary and low contrast. The product screens do not use it.

## Motion

Things **settle**. A confident ease-out, around 220 ms, no bounce and no spin. Hover deepens the field by one step or lifts to mint. Press scales to 0.97 with no color flash.

<MotionScale />

| Event | Duration | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Press | `dur-fast` 120 ms | Scale 0.97 |
| Hover, color, small moves | `dur-base` 220 ms | |
| Panels, dialogs, entrances | `dur-slow` 420 ms | Fade and a short rise |

With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every duration drops to 0 and the press scale to 1. Never carry meaning in motion alone.

## Focus

Focus is a **teal ring with an amber halo** (mint on dark). The amber alone is 1.9:1 on white and fails; the teal ring clears 3:1 on every surface, and the halo keeps the brand.
