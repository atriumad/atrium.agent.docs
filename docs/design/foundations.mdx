# *Foundations*: drawn, not shaded

## Spacing

A **4 px grid**. Space between related things is small, space between groups is large; sections breathe with 64 to 88 px.

<SpaceScale />

| Where | Gap |
| --- | --- |
| Icon to label | 4 to 8 px |
| Label to field | 8 px |
| Between fields | 16 px |
| Card padding | 24 px by default, 16 px for a list row |
| Between cards | 16 px |
| Between page sections | 64 to 88 px, with a 3 px rule |

## Layout

A **12-column grid**, content at most **1240 px** (1440 for wide dashboards), a gutter of `clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)`. Text stays under **68 characters** a line. The Manager and the Platform show a side navigation from `lg` upward.

<Breakpoints />

Design mobile first. A table that cannot fit becomes a list of cards, never a horizontal scroll of the page.

## Borders and the rule

The system is drawn, not shaded. Borders do the work shadows would do elsewhere.

<BordersTable />

The **3 px rule** is structural: it appears on nearly every section boundary and above every table. The 1 px hairline separates rows inside a block.

## Elevation

Elevation is **adjacency and rules**, not blur. A default card is cream-100 with a 6 px radius, a 1 px hairline and no shadow. The one card that matters gets a 1 px ink border and the **2 px printed offset**. A diffuse shadow belongs only to floating UI.

<ShadowScale />

<Demo surface="light">
  <div style={{display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: 'repeat(auto-fit, minmax(180px, 1fr))', gap: 16, width: '100%'}}>
    <Card title="Paper">Hairline, flat.</Card>
    <Card tone="ruled" elevation="offset" title="Ruled, offset">The printed drop.</Card>

  </div>

There are **no gradients, no mesh, no glass and no grain overlay**. Imagery sits inside a card with a hairline; text over an image sits in a solid charcoal capsule, never a protection gradient.

## Motion

Quiet and quick. A confident ease-out, three durations, **fades and 1 to 2 px moves only**. No bounce, no spring, no scale-in.

<MotionScale />

| State | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Hover | The fill deepens one step; an outline button inverts to solid; a card lifts 2 px and its border goes to ink; a link turns clay and thickens its underline to 2 px |
| Press | `translateY(1px)`. Never a scale, never a color flash |
| Focus | A 2 px clay outline at 2 px offset, on everything focusable |
| Disabled | Opacity 0.4, no color change |

With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every duration drops to 0 and the press shift to 0. Motion never carries the only signal.

## Focus

Clay on cream is 3.5:1 and on charcoal 3.8:1, so the 2 px clay ring clears the 3:1 an interface part needs on both.
