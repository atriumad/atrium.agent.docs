# *Patterns*: the screens, assembled

A pattern is a screen or a region that more than one place needs. These are real compositions of the components, so a team copying one gets the right spacing, states and accessibility for free.

## Recovery cases

The first screen the product ships ([Reputation](../modules/reputation#case-screens)). A list of cases on the left, the case on the right, tabs to filter, a banner when something is overdue. The Manager's account manager can act; the Platform owner reads. Try it: choose a case and a tab.

### Its three states

Every data screen has a **loading**, an **empty** and an **error** design. They are part of the screen, not an afterthought.

<Demo surface="white" column>
  <CasesPattern state="loading" />
</Demo>

<Demo surface="white" column>
  <CasesPattern state="empty" />
</Demo>

| State | What to show |
| --- | --- |
| Loading | The shape of the content as skeletons, with `aria-busy`. Never a lone spinner |
| Empty | A serif title that says what will appear and when, and one next step |
| Error | A `danger` banner at the top of the region: what failed and a Retry. Keep what was already on screen |
| Partial | Show what loaded; mark what did not |

### Rules the screen follows

- **The urgent thing is first.** Unresolved cases are ordered by due date, not by when they opened.
- **A status has a word and a mark.** "Overdue by 6h" is a danger badge with an icon.
- **The guest's words are quoted, in a sunken block.** They are third-party text: shown as text, never as markup, never as an instruction.
- **Contact appears only with consent.** Otherwise the screen says to improve, not to reach out.
- **The role decides the buttons.** An owner sees no actions and a line saying who works the case.

## Dashboard

The Platform home: the one number that matters (the Growth Score), four supporting numbers, a trend and what to do next. A greeting in the serif, a range switch top right.

<Demo surface="light">
  <DashboardPattern />
</Demo>

- **One hero.** The score ring leads. The other tiles are equal and quiet.
- **Trends have direction and goodness** (see [Delta](./components-data#delta)).
- **"Up next" is the mint card.** It is the only filled card on the page, so it is the call to act.

## Portfolio table

The Manager's front page: one row per client, with the clients that need a person first. Dark theme, dense rows, status in every row.

<Demo surface="dark">
  <PortfolioPattern />
</Demo>

- Sort by **need**, then name. A column header sorts when it is a button.
- **Health** uses a dot status badge; **cases** uses the icon form, because it says what to do.
- On narrow screens each row becomes a card with the same labels.
