# *Surfaces*: one system, four rooms

One set of tokens and components, themed per room. A surface changes **roles** (what is the page, what is the card, what is the accent), never the components.

| | Platform | Manager | Hub | Mobile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **For** | The restaurant owner | The agency team | The restaurant's guest | The owner on the go |
| **Theme** | Light | Dark chrome, light work area | Light, per restaurant | Light |
| **Density** | Calm: 16 px body, roomy cards | Dense: 14 px, tables | Spacious, one task | Large targets, one decision |
| **Page** | `surface-page` cloud | `surface-page` cloud, side nav teal | The restaurant's brand field | `surface-page` cloud |
| **Accent** | Mint and amber | Mint, amber count badge | The restaurant's color | Mint |
| **Numbers** | Serif | Sans with tabular numerals | Serif for the big figure | Serif |

## Platform

Calm and personal. The greeting is in the serif, the score leads, and every tile is a card with room to breathe. The only filled card is the "Up next" call.

## Manager

Dense and fast. A teal side navigation, tables with a status in every row, and an **amber count** for the work waiting on a person. See the [portfolio pattern](./patterns#portfolio-table).

## Hub: a restaurant's own colors

The Hub, the survey and public pages take each restaurant's **accent color and logo** on top of the same components. Two guards keep that safe:

1. **The accent replaces `--accent` only.** Text stays teal or ink; the accent never becomes text on white.
2. **A contrast check at setup.** The accent must give 4.5:1 for its label color (the system picks teal or white, whichever passes) and 3:1 against the page. If it fails, the system darkens it until it does and tells the account manager.

## Mobile

The owner checks in for a minute. One decision per screen, **44 px targets**, the figure that matters in the serif, push for what needs them. Same tokens, larger type (base 16, headings one step up) and more space.

## Agents

An agent appears wherever it acts or speaks: a face (Blobatar), a name and a card color. The colors are its **identity**, so it never takes a status color, and a status never takes an agent's.
